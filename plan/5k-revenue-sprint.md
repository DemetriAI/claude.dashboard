- **Goal:** $5,000 cash in 4–5 days.

## Read this first — the cash engine

Your offer is a **14-day _free_ pilot**. Free pilot = $0 on day one = the pilot itself produces **no sprint cash**. To bank $5K in five days, the money has to come from a **launch / build fee collected to begin**:

> *"$1,800 to stand up your 4 workflows. Then 14 days free. Then $650/mo."*

Anchor that $1,800 against the **$130K–$180K/yr leak the teardown just showed them** — it's a rounding error on the problem. **If you truly charge $0 to start, $5K-in-5-days is not reachable from this funnel** — the launch fee is the one non-negotiable for the timeline. (Pricing alternatives that still pull cash forward are listed below.)

## Locking in the 99% — the 3 load-bearing assumptions

The math isn't the risk; these three are. Here's how each gets nailed:

1. **Send volume (biggest risk).** 60–110 personalized videos in 4 days sounds heavy. → **De-risk:** I batch-generate every script and subject line from the verify pipeline, so it collapses into a *recording* task (2 sittings), not a writing task.
2. **Reply rate (15% est.).** Driven entirely by real personalization — their name, **their phone ringing on camera**, a named competitor, and their **$ leak in the subject line**. → **De-risk:** keep the ring-the-number beat; lead the subject with the dollar figure.
3. **Call-close (60% est.).** It's a free pilot, so the "close" is really *"pay the launch fee to start now."* → **De-risk:** anchor $1,800 against the six-figure leak; offer *"pay today, we build this week."*
