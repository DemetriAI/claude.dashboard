# Vertical Skin — accounting

> Same 12 agents, accounting-specific pains/guardrails. Same **missed-call → lost client** spine as
> optometry, plus two pains unique to this vertical: **seasonal call spikes** and the **document chase**.

## ICP / who we keep
Owner-operated CPA / tax / bookkeeping firm (solo–~20 staff) · recurring + seasonal client base · the
**owner owns the phone + the onboarding workflow**. **HALT:** Big 4 / national chains / firms with a
staffed central client-service desk.

## Pains (TIME or MONEY, quantified — est., confirm on the call)
| Rank | Pain | Economics (est.) | AI fix (intake/scheduling/doc-collection only) | First result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Missed/slow calls, worst in tax season** → lost clients | Retained client ~$1,500–$5,000/yr. Recover ~3–8 clients/yr off missed calls → **~$5,000–$15,000/yr+**, concentrated in season | AI answers every call 24/7, qualifies, books the consult | ≤7 days |
| 2 | **Client onboarding doc chase** (PBC lists, missing forms) → staff hours bleed, refunds delayed | ~2–5 hrs/client of back-and-forth × staff rate; weeks added to turnaround | AI requests, chases, and collects documents on a schedule | ≤7 days |
| 3 | **Consult/appointment no-shows** | Lost billable slots | Automated reminders + reschedule | ≤7 days |
| 4 | **Slow web-lead response** | Speed-to-lead decides who signs | AI answers + books inbound leads instantly | ≤7 days |
| 5 | **Review leakage** | Thin reviews lose discovery | AI review request post-engagement | ≤7 days |

## Off-limits / guardrails
- **The AI never gives tax or financial advice.** Intake, scheduling, and document collection only.
- Financial data is sensitive — lead the compliance objection with **confidentiality + NDA**, data stays theirs.

## Channels & agent deltas
- **Cold (Agent 7):** email + LinkedIn + phone, Loom-led. Viable. Time campaigns around pre-season.
- **Warm (Agent 6):** referral networks, local business associations, bank/loan-officer referrals.
- **Ads (Agent 8):** viable seasonally once the 2-client gate is met.
- **Value per unit:** retained client / engagement; lead with annual client value + season-spike pain.
- **Close (downstream):** `./calls/_new-prep.sh "<Firm>" legal` (legal/financial confidentiality skin).
