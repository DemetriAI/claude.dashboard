# Pain Briefs — law (Agent 3)

Conservative, confirm-on-the-call estimates. **Value unit: a signed matter is $3,500+** (often far more
for PI). The wedge across every PROCEED firm is the same: injury/criminal/immigration intake and
estate/family consults arrive **after-hours or while the owner-attorney is in court** — and a missed
intake call is a client who retains the next firm. The AI does **intake + scheduling only (never legal
advice)**; lead the compliance objection with **confidentiality + privilege + NDA**.

Gate result: **23 PROCEED · 5 HALT**.

## PROCEED — top targets

### 1. Loewy Law Firm — Austin (92)
- **Pain:** Solo-led PI firm, deliberately small docket and small intake staff. PI calls land 24/7 and the first firm to answer usually signs; after-hours/lunch/second-caller intakes roll to voicemail.
- **Economics:** Signed PI matter $3,500+ → recovering 2–6 missed intakes/mo ≈ **$7,000–$25,000/mo (est.)**.
- **AI fix:** 24/7 AI intake — qualifies (no legal advice), books the consult; live on their number ≤7 days.

### 2. McCoy Family Law — Denver (91)
- **Pain:** Solo owner-attorney in court/mediation = an unanswered line; family-law callers shop fast and call the next firm.
- **Economics:** Family matter $3,500+ → **$7,000–$17,500/mo (est.)**.
- **AI fix:** 24/7 intake that captures the matter and books the consult while she's in court.

### 3. Pardo Law Firm — Charlotte (90)
- **Pain:** 2-attorney immigration firm, voted top-3 in Charlotte 8 years running — award visibility drives call volume (often bilingual, after-hours) into a tiny office.
- **Economics:** Retained immigration matter $3,500+ → **$3,500–$14,000/mo (est.)**.
- **AI fix:** 24/7 **bilingual** intake — qualifies, books the consult.

### 4. Bonilla Law Firm — Austin (90)
- **Pain:** Family-run PI PLLC, two primary attorneys; injury calls land nights/weekends with no big intake team behind the line.
- **Economics:** **$7,000–$25,000/mo (est.)** on recovered PI intakes.
- **AI fix:** 24/7 intake line, books the consult, live ≤7 days.

### 5. Mark Scruggs, Attorney at Law — Nashville (89)

- **Economics:** Retained criminal matter $3,500+ → **$7,000–$17,500/mo (est.)**.

### 6. Buitrago Law Firm — Tampa (89)
