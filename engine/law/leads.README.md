# Leads — law (method & scoring)

**Source:** web research. Apollo people **and** company search are paywalled on the current plan
(0 credits charged), so this list is web-verified, mirroring the approach in
`../optometry/leads.README.md`. Every firm is real with a real website; unverified specifics are tagged
`[verify]`; contact PII is deliberately kept out (use the website form / main line).

**ICP:** owner-operated US firms, **solo–~10 attorneys**, consumer-facing intake — personal injury,
family, criminal, estate/probate, immigration. The owner owns the intake line.

**Scoring rubric (0–100):**
- **+** owner-operator with a small/thin intake staff
- **+** urgent or consumer practice area where speed-to-lead decides who signs (PI, criminal, immigration)
- **+** after-hours / weekend / **bilingual** call demand
- **−/HALT** BigLaw, staffed 24/7 intake call centers, multi-state platform firms, in-house legal depts

**Pain model:** missed/after-hours intake → lost signed matters. **Value unit: a signed matter is
$3,500+** (PI often far more), so even a few recovered intakes/month is large.

**Result:** 28 rows — **23 PROCEED / 5 HALT**, spread across Austin, Denver, Nashville, Tampa,
Charlotte, and Phoenix. Decision-maker names are the owner/founding attorney where verifiable; confirm
the exact name before outreach.
