# Follow-Up Cadence — after the letters land

A letter alone rarely closes; the reply comes from the *touches after it*. This is a 14-day, multi-channel cadence keyed off the letter. Keep it compliant: business email with a real opt-out (CAN-SPAM), 1:1 personal calls are fine, and **no automated SMS** — only a personal, manually-sent text if you already have a relationship/consent.

> Timing assumes the letter lands ~Day 0 (3–4 business days after mailing). Track each scan via the QR `?ref=` tag — **a scan = warm, prioritize that person.**

## The cadence

| Day | Channel | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Letter arrives | First impression + free-value offer + QR to demo |
| 3 | **Email #1** | Reference the letter, restate the free audit, link the demo |
| 6 | **Call** (or LinkedIn DM if no number) | Human touch; offer the 15 min |
| 9 | **Email #2** | One new proof point + a softer ask |
| 14 | **Email #3 (break-up)** | "Should I close your file?" — often the highest reply rate |

## Compliance notes
- **Email:** include a real opt-out and your physical mailing address (CAN-SPAM). Honor opt-outs immediately.
- **Calls:** 1:1 cold calls to business lines are fine; scrub against the DNC if calling personal cells; keep records.
- **Text:** do **not** automate SMS to these prospects without prior express consent (TCPA). A single, personally-typed text is a judgment call — keep it 1:1, not blasted.
