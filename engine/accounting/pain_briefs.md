# Pain Briefs — accounting (Agent 3)

Conservative, confirm-on-the-call estimates. **Value unit: a retained client is ~$1,500–$5,000/yr** of
recurring revenue. Two wedges across every PROCEED firm: **(a) missed calls spike hard in tax season**
(Jan–Apr) — a missed caller signs with the next firm and that's a multi-year client gone; **(b) the
onboarding document chase** (PBC lists, organizers, missing forms) burns **~2–5 hrs/client** of staff
time and stretches turnaround. The AI does **intake + scheduling + document collection only (never tax
or financial advice)**; lead the compliance objection with **confidentiality + NDA — data stays theirs**.

Gate result: **22 PROCEED · 5 HALT**.

## PROCEED — top targets

### 1. Lowy's Tax Planning & Accounting — Phoenix (91)
- **Pain:** Owner-founded (2002), small team carrying **300+ small-business clients and ~600 individual returns/yr** — Jan–Apr the phone and the organizer/doc chase both bury the office at once.
- **Economics:** Recovering even 1–2 lost callers/mo in season + cutting the doc chase across ~900 engagements ≈ **$2,500–$6,000/mo (est.)**.
- **AI fix:** 24/7 AI front desk — answers, qualifies (no tax advice), books consults, runs the document chase on a schedule; live on their number ≤7 days.
