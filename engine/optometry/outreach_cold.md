# Cold Outreach — optometry (Agent 7)

Lead-with-pain, artifact-led. Every message opens with **their** pain + the **$ figure**, has **one
CTA** (book 15 min), and carries the **personalized Loom** as the value artifact. No spray-and-pray.

**Rule of 100:** design for ~100 personalized touches/day — but *personalized* (each carries that
practice's Loom + pain band), never blasted. Quality of the artifact is the unlock.

**Daily mechanics:** ~10–15 fresh targets/day from `leads.csv` (top score first) → record a Loom from
`looms/_TEMPLATE.md` → run the 5-touch cadence below. Log replies to `pipeline.csv` → hand to
`bookings.md` (Agent 9).

## The 5-touch cadence (per target, ~9 business days)
| Day | Channel | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Email #1** + Loom | Pain + $ figure + the free-pilot CTA |
| 2 | **Phone** (opener below) | Catch them live; reference the Loom |
| 4 | **LinkedIn** connect + 1-line note | Second surface, low friction |
| 6 | **Email #2** (bump) | Risk-reversal; "you only continue if it works" |
| 9 | **Email #3** (breakup) | Takeaway close; leaves the door open |

## Worked example — Round Rock Eyes (pain band $3,000–$6,500/mo)

**Email #1** — subject: `Round Rock Eyes is losing patients at 6pm`
> Dr. `[[owner]]` — when your desk is with a patient or it's after hours, your calls roll to
> voicemail, and in eye care that caller just books down 620. Conservatively that's **~$3,000–$6,500/mo**
> in exams + eyewear walking out.
> I made you a 90-second teardown: `[[Loom link]]`.
> The fix: an AI front desk that answers every call 24/7 and books it. I'll build it **free** and run
> it on your phones for 7 days — you only continue if it catches calls.
> Worth 15 minutes this week? `[[booking link]]`

**Phone opener (Day 2):**
> "Hi, this is `[[you]]` — I sent Dr. `[[owner]]` a quick 90-second video on the calls Round Rock Eyes
> misses after hours. Conservatively it's a few thousand a month in bookings. I'm not selling
> anything today — I just want 15 minutes to show the fix and offer to build it free. Is the doctor
> around, or when's good?"

**LinkedIn note (Day 4):**
> Dr. `[[owner]]` — sent you a 90-sec teardown of Round Rock Eyes' missed-call leak (~$3–6.5K/mo). Happy
> to build the fix free for 7 days. Worth a look?

**Email #2 — bump (Day 6)** — subject: `re: Round Rock Eyes`
> Floating this back up. The whole point of the free 7-day pilot is you risk nothing — it runs on your
> real phones and you only pay if it's catching calls. 15 minutes? `[[booking link]]`

**Email #3 — breakup (Day 9)** — subject: `closing the loop`
> I'll stop here, Dr. `[[owner]]` — but the leak doesn't stop on its own. If catching every after-hours
> call ever moves up your list, the free pilot stands. `[[booking link]]`
