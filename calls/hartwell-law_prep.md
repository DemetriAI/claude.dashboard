## 1 · Qualification (5 questions)

1. **Volume —** "Walk me through a typical week — how many intake calls come in, and how many do you think aren't getting answered live while you and the team are in court or with clients?"
   - *Anchor:* "Teardown showed roughly 90 a week, a real chunk during court hours — track?" → *Follow:* "What's an average signed matter worth to the firm?"

2. **Current handling —** "When an intake call comes in during a deposition, or at 8pm, what happens to it right now? And how are you handling consult no-shows?"
   - *Listening for:* answering service that messages but doesn't book → slow callback → lead lost to a faster firm. That's the bleed.

3. **Decision-maker —** "If this is a fit, is it your call, or would you bring your intake lead / partners in?"

4. **Timeline —** "If we could have every intake call answered and screened inside two weeks, is now the time to fix it, or is something ahead of it?"

5. **Budget authority —** "One extra signed case a month dwarfs this. When something pays for itself, can you greenlight it, or is there a partner conversation first?"

## 4 · Objection handling

**"I'm in court all week — I don't have time for this."**
- "That's the exact problem we solve — you being unreachable is when the leads call the next firm. And **we build it, you don't.** Fifteen minutes now stops cases walking out the door while you're in trial."

**"We already use an answering service."**
- "Then you've felt it — they take a message, they don't screen the matter or book the consult, and by the time someone calls back the lead has signed elsewhere. This answers live 24/7, screens, and books the consult on the spot. What happens to an 8pm PI call right now?"

**"I'm not sure about AI handling sensitive intake."**
- "Fair — and that's the pilot's whole job: you don't trust my word, you hear it handle *your* real intake calls for two weeks, free. If the tone or screening isn't right for your firm, you don't convert."

**"It's too expensive."**
- "Against one signed case? A single PI matter is multiples of a year of this. You're losing at least a couple of signable leads a month to slow callbacks — recovering *one* pays for the whole year. And you pay nothing until it's proven."

**"What about client confidentiality / privilege?"**
- **Acknowledge + flip:** "Exactly the right question for a firm — confidentiality is the whole game."
- **Evidence:** "Caller and matter information is handled strictly confidentially — encrypted, access-logged — and we sign an NDA / confidentiality agreement up front. Our infrastructure is healthcare-grade (BAA-ready), so it's built for privileged data."
- **Re-close:** "Confidentiality's covered in writing. The exposure today is intake calls hitting voicemail and walking to the firm down the street — shall we stop that?"
