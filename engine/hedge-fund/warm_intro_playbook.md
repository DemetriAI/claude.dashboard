# Warm-Intro Playbook — hedge_fund (the ONLY entry path)

> **This vertical has no `leads.csv` and never will.** Cold-blasting principals is off-limits; ads are
> **STOP** (Agent 8). The unit of work here is an **introduction path**, not a lead. The ask is always
> the **introduction — never the sale.** No warm path to a fund → mark it `intro-required` and HALT.

---

## 1) Who we ask to be introduced to (the buyer — never the PM)

| Fund size (est. AUM) | Titles to ask for | Why them |
|---|---|---|
| < $250M (emerging) | COO (often COO/CFO/CCO in one person) | Wears every ops hat; feels DDQ pain personally |
| $250M–$2B | COO · CFO · Director of Operations · Head of IR · CCO | Lean team, institutional allocator demands — the sweet spot |
| > $2B | Head of Investor Relations · RFP/DDQ manager · Compliance Operations lead | Dedicated roles exist; longer vendor gate |

**Never ask to meet the PM/CIO.** If a connector only knows the PM, the ask becomes: *"Could you ask
them who runs ops/IR — and whether they'd connect us to that person?"*

## 2) Which funds fit (target by TYPE / strategy / size — not by name)
Best fit — probe for these traits, in order:
1. **Institutional-allocator-facing:** pensions, endowments, foundations, funds-of-funds, or consultants
   in the LP base → recurring DDQs/RFPs/database updates by definition.
2. **Lean ops bench:** roughly **$100M–$2B single-manager funds** — big enough to receive serious
   diligence, too small for a dedicated RFP team.
3. **In or entering a raise:** emerging managers post-seed, funds launching a new vehicle, or anyone
   "in the consultant databases" — questionnaire volume spikes exactly there.
4. **Strategy-agnostic** (equity L/S, credit, macro, event, quant): the *operational* paperwork is the
   same; we never engage the strategy itself.

> Illustrative only: household-name mega-managers (the Citadel / Bridgewater tier of the world) are
> **NOT targets** — they run internal RFP teams and year-long vendor gates. Named here purely to mark
> the ceiling of the profile, not as prospects.

## 3) Where introductions come from (connector map — work top-down)
| Rank | Connector type | Why they can intro |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Your own network:** former colleagues, classmates, friends in fund ops/IR/compliance | Trust already exists — one of these outranks everything below |
| 2 | **Fund service providers:** administrators, fund-formation / regulatory counsel, audit teams, compliance consultants | They sit inside the ops workflow and know exactly who drowns in DDQs |
| 3 | **ODD / allocator-side contacts** | They *send* the DDQs; they know which managers struggle to turn them around |
| 4 | **Industry community:** AIMA / MFA events, CFA society, alternatives conferences, alumni groups | Slower, but compounding — be the "governed AI for fund ops" person there |
| 5 | **First pilot champion → peers** | Post-pilot: "Which one ops/COO friend should see this?" |

Log every path in this folder as `intro_map.md` rows: connector → target role → fund profile → status
(`asked / opted-in / introduced / no-path`). **PII stays in `_private/`, never in tracked files.**

## 4) Give-first (always lead with one of these — never the pitch)
1. **The synthetic demo** — `pilot_ddq.md`: a full DDQ-automation walkthrough on fictional data.
   Forwardable, zero-risk, shows we get the NDA/security reality before anyone asks.
2. **Answer-library hygiene checklist** — a short, free teardown framework: where approved answers
   live, who owns versions, how stale the library is. Useful even if they never buy.
3. **The governance stance** — our NDA-first / security-review-welcome / no-training-on-your-data
   posture (in `pilot_ddq.md`). In this vertical, *that* is the credibility asset.

**Give to the connector too:** a forwardable blurb (below) so the intro costs them 60 seconds, and a
report-back afterward so they look good for making it.

## 5) Rules of the ask
- **Double opt-in always:** connector checks with the ops lead first; we are forwarded, never imposed.
- One ask per connector per quarter. No attachments containing anything sensitive. No pricing talk.
- Never imply the fund "needs" us — the frame is *"if this is their week-eating problem, I'd love to
  meet them."*
- Expectation-set honestly: demo on synthetic material → their NDA → their security review → pilot.
  **Never promise a fast close; only fast delivery (≤7 days, synthetic).**
