# Warm Outreach — law (Agent 6)

**Status for this run:** every target in `leads.csv` is **cold** (web-sourced, no prior relationship),
so there are no warm contacts to send *yet*. This file is the **ready system** — it activates the
moment you have a warm contact, plus plays to manufacture warmth. Law is the most referral-wired
vertical in the engine: attorneys already trade conflict referrals and out-of-practice-area handoffs
daily, so one good intro compounds fast.

**Framework:** give-give-give-ask + **ACA** (Acknowledge, Compliment, Ask). Never hard-pitch.
**Rules:** 3 touches/contact · single CTA · **no pricing in T1** · <120 words/msg.
**Guardrail in all copy:** the AI gives **no legal advice** — intake + scheduling only —
**confidentiality + privilege + NDA** led up front.

---

## Where warm contacts come from (work these first — highest conversion in the engine)
1. **Pilot clients → peer attorneys.** After a good 7-day result, ask: *"Who's the one attorney you
   refer matters to when you're conflicted out?"* Conflict-referral networks are pre-built trust.
2. **Bar associations — local + practice-area sections.** CLE events, solo/small-firm sections,
   young-lawyers divisions. One credible member vouching for you opens a dozen doors. Give first:
   offer a free "what your intake line misses" teardown to the section, no pitch.
3. **Your existing network.** Anyone who knows a solo or small-firm owner — one intro outranks 50
   cold emails.
4. **Adjacent vendors.** Legal-marketing agencies, case-management consultants (Clio/MyCase
   ecosystems), court reporters, legal staffing recruiters — they know every owner in town and trade
   intros.
5. **Law-school alumni groups.** Same vouch dynamic as the bar sections.

> Log each warm contact as a new row in `pipeline.csv` (stage `warm_contact`) so it doesn't get lost.

## The 3-touch warm sequence (fill and send)

**T1 — value first (ACA, no ask for the sale).** *Give.*
> Hey {{NAME}} — {{MUTUAL}} mentioned you run {{FIRM}} **[Acknowledge]**. I've been deep in how
> solo and small firms stop losing intake calls while they're in court or after hours, and what
> you've built is exactly the kind of firm that deserves to catch every one **[Compliment]**. I made
> you a 90-second teardown of where I think intakes are leaking — want me to send it over?
> **[Ask: permission, not pitch]**

**T2 — short re-engage (2–3 days later).** *Give again.*
> Quick follow-up, {{NAME}} — here's that teardown: {{LOOM_LINK}}. One number jumped out: at $3,500+
> per signed matter, it looks like ~{{PAIN_BAND}}/mo in missed intakes. (And to pre-empt the obvious
> question — the fix does intake + scheduling only, never legal advice, under NDA.) No agenda — tell
> me if I'm off base.

**T3 — the ask (free pilot OR the intro, risk-reversed).** *Ask.*
> If it's useful, I'll build the fix for {{FIRM}} **free** and run it on your real intake line for
> 7 days — confidentiality and privilege built in, you only continue if it catches intakes. Worth 15
> minutes? **And if it's not for you but you know the solo or small firm it *is* for — who comes to
> mind?** {{BOOKING_LINK}}

## Bar-association play (manufactured warmth, give-first)
1. Join the local bar's **solo & small firm section** (cheap, often open to affiliates/vendors).
2. **Give:** offer the section chair a free 20-min talk or a written one-pager — *"The after-hours
   intake leak: what a missed call costs a small firm"* — numbers only, zero pitch.
3. **Give:** free intake-line teardowns for any member who asks (these become Looms).
4. **Ask (later):** the chair/host intro — *"who in the section is drowning in missed intake?"*

---
*VERIFY: 3 touches · single CTA each · no pricing in T1 · <120 words/msg · no-legal-advice +
confidentiality/privilege/NDA present. For a referral target, the ask in T3 is the **introduction**,
not the sale.*
