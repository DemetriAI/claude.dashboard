**only** entry path (cold ≈ noise, Agent 8 ads = **STOP**). The sequence below runs **problem-first

> Genuinely curious whether it matches what you see.

**T2 — give again (3–4 days later, with the artifact).**
> Here's that walkthrough: {{LINK to pilot_ddq.md content}}. Two honest notes: the demo runs on
> **entirely synthetic data** — no fund's real answers anywhere in it — and we expect NDA + security
> review before any real document. Teams estimate hours-to-days per questionnaire today; this turns
> most of it into review-and-approve. If anything reads wrong to you, tell me — that's more useful

**T3 — the ask = the INTRODUCTION (3–4 days later).**
> {{NAME}} — one real ask, fully fine to pass. Is there **one COO / head of IR / compliance lead** you
