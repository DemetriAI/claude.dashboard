# Vertical Skin — real_estate

> Same 12 agents, real-estate-specific pains/guardrails. The spine is **speed-to-lead → lost commission**:
> these agents already **buy** leads (Meta/Google ads) but can't answer fast enough, so the spend leaks.
> The cold open is their own ad spend.

## ICP / who we keep
Individual agent, small team, or small independent brokerage **actively running Meta/Google ads** · the
**agent/team lead owns the phone + the ad budget** · visible lead-gen funnels / landing pages / IDX
capture. **HALT:** mega-teams with a staffed ISA (inside-sales) call center, large franchise offices with
central lead routing, iBuyers/portals (Zillow, Opendoor, Redfin) — they already have speed-to-lead solved.

## Pains (TIME or MONEY, quantified — est., confirm on the call)
| Rank | Pain | Economics (est.) | AI fix (intake / qualify / schedule only) | First result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Slow speed-to-lead on PAID leads** → ad spend leaks | Buy-side commission ~$6K–$15K/deal; Meta/portal leads die in minutes. 1–2 saved deals/yr = **$6K–$30K** + the wasted monthly ad spend | AI answers, qualifies (budget/timeline/financing), and books the showing in <1 min, 24/7 | ≤7 days |
| 2 | **Missed calls while showing / after hours** → buyer calls the next sign | Every unanswered call is a potential commission to a competitor | AI answers every call, books, texts the agent the hot ones | ≤7 days |
| 3 | **No follow-up on aged leads** | A CRM full of cold paid leads never reworked | AI reactivation campaign to the old lead DB | ≤7 days |
| 4 | **Showing no-shows** | Wasted drive time + lost slots | Automated reminders + easy reschedule | ≤7 days |

## Off-limits / guardrails
- **Fair Housing Act (non-negotiable):** the AI never screens, steers, or answers based on protected
  classes (race, color, religion, sex, familial status, national origin, disability). Intake + scheduling
  + qualification on **budget / timeline / financing** only.
- **No pricing, offer, or legal advice** on a property — the AI routes those straight to the agent.

## Channels & agent deltas
- **Cold (Agent 7):** email + SMS + phone, Loom-led — opens on **their ad spend leaking**. Strongest hook in the engine.
- **Warm (Agent 6):** lender / title / mortgage-broker referral networks; past-client ACA.
- **Ads (Agent 8):** viable once 2 paying clients — and you can **dogfood** (run the AI on your own ad leads as proof).
- **Value per unit:** a closed transaction (commission); lead with lost commissions **plus** the monthly ad spend they're already burning.
- **Close (downstream):** `./calls/_new-prep.sh "<Team>" home` (home-services speed-to-lead skin).
