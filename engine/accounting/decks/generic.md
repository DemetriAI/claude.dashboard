# Gamma deck — generic accounting (reusable)

Use when you don't have a personalized deck for a target yet. Swap `{{FIRM}}` / `{{METRO}}` /
`{{PAIN_BAND}}` and paste into **Gamma → Generate**. Problem-first. Card 1 = pain. One CTA.
Guardrail card (4) is mandatory.

---
```text
Create a 7-card presentation for a live 15-minute call with {{FIRM}}, an owner-operated
accounting/tax firm in {{METRO}}. Tone: direct, plain-spoken, numbers-first, no hype. Problem-first
— do NOT open with our company or features. One call to action, last card only.

Card 1 — The leak: "{{FIRM}} loses ~{{PAIN_BAND}} every year to busy-season missed calls."
From mid-January to April 15 the phone rings 3–4x normal — at exactly the moment everyone is
heads-down in returns. Overflow, lunch, and after-6 calls roll to voicemail, and a prospect who hits
voicemail in March signs with the next firm. A retained client is $1,500–$5,000/yr — and renews.
(Conservative estimate; confirmed together on the call.)

Card 2 — Why it persists: "It's a coverage problem, not a staff problem." No firm can staff for a
12-week spike, and the same staff hours are bleeding into the document chase — 2–5 hours per client
of PBC-list back-and-forth before the real work can even start. Hiring doesn't fix simultaneity or
seasonality.

Card 3 — The fix: "An AI front desk that answers every call, books it, and chases the documents."
24/7, sounds like your firm, qualifies the caller, books the consult into your calendar — and runs
your document requests on a schedule (call + text follow-ups until every item lands).

Card 4 — Guardrails first: "No tax or financial advice. Ever." The AI does intake, scheduling, and
document collection only — it never interprets a return or recommends a position. NDA signed before
we touch your line. Your clients' financial data stays confidential and stays yours.

Card 5 — What 7 days looks like: "Live on your number in 7 days." Forward the line, train it on your
services + scheduling rules + a real document checklist, then it answers real calls and chases real
PBC lists. Caught calls and faster doc turnaround show inside the week — ready before the next
deadline (Oct 15 or Jan 15), no IT project, no seasonal hire.

Card 6 — Pilot terms + proof: "Free 7-day pilot. You'll be the proof — on your own phones." Built
free, price locked in the agreement you sign now, charged only if you keep it after the pilot proves
itself. If it doesn't catch calls or speed up the chase, you walk, owe nothing. The evidence is your
own calendar and your own inbox.

Card 7 — One CTA: "Let's set it live this week." Pick a 15-minute slot; we confirm your number,
services, and document checklist; it's answering within days. One button: Book the 15 minutes.
```
---
*VERIFY: Card 1 = the pain (retained-client $/yr) · one CTA card · guardrail card present (no
tax/financial advice + confidentiality/NDA) · 7 cards · no alpha claims (N/A).*
