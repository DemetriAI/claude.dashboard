# Leads — accounting (method & scoring)

**Source:** web research. Apollo people **and** company search are paywalled on the current plan
(0 credits charged), so this list is web-verified, mirroring the approach in
`../optometry/leads.README.md` and `../law/leads.README.md`. Every firm is real with a real website
(verified via the firm's own site and/or 2+ corroborating sources — Yelp/BBB/LinkedIn/chambers; many
small-CPA site hosts 403-block fetchers, so corroboration was used where direct fetch failed).
Unverified specifics are tagged `[verify]`; contact PII is deliberately kept out — decision-maker is
the owner/managing CPA only where it is the firm's own public identity (usually the firm name), and
the contact path is always the website form / main line.

**ICP:** owner-operated US CPA / tax / bookkeeping firms, **solo–~20 staff**, recurring + seasonal
client base — the owner owns the phone and the onboarding workflow.

**Scoring rubric (0–100):**
- **+** solo/owner-operated with a thin or nonexistent front desk
- **+** heavy tax-prep book (organizer/PBC season = call + doc-chase spike Jan–Apr)
- **+** bookkeeping/payroll recurring clients (monthly touch = constant inbound)
- **+** multi-site small firm (call routing pain), niche visibility that drives call volume
- **−/HALT** Big 4, national chains (H&R Block etc.), national virtual/distributed platforms, firms
  with a staffed central client-service desk, or >~20 staff

**Pain model (two wedges):** (a) **missed calls spike hard in tax season** → a missed caller retains
the next firm; a retained client is **~$1,500–$5,000/yr** recurring, so a few lost callers compound
fast; (b) **onboarding document chase** (PBC lists, organizers) burns **~2–5 hrs/client** of staff
time. `pain_monthly_est` blends both, conservatively, marked "(est.)" — confirm on the call.
Guardrail: **the AI never gives tax or financial advice** — intake, scheduling, and document
collection only; lead compliance objections with confidentiality + NDA.

**Result:** 27 rows — **22 PROCEED / 5 HALT**, spread across Austin (4), Denver (4), Nashville (3),
Tampa (4), Charlotte (3), Phoenix (4); HALTs cover Big 4, national chain, central-desk virtual firm,
national distributed platform, and an over-ICP local platform. Confirm decision-maker names and firm
size before outreach.
