## 0 · Teardown snapshot (read before you dial)

- **Call volume:** ~180 inbound/week
- **The leak:** ~22% hit voicemail (~40 calls/wk); evenings + weekends ring out with **no callback**
- **No-show / cancellation handling today:** front desk calls back manually "when there's time"; **no automated reminders; ~15 no-shows/mo**
- **What a booked appointment is worth:** ~$450 / new-patient visit (lifetime value often $1,200+)
- **Estimated monthly revenue leaking:** ~$10–14K/mo (missed new-patient calls + unfilled no-show slots)

## 1 · Qualification (5 questions)

1. **Volume —** "Walk me through a typical week — roughly how many calls come in, and honestly, how many do you think hit voicemail or never get answered?"
   - *Anchor to teardown:* "I clocked about 40 a week going to voicemail — does that track?" → *Follow:* "And one new patient is worth what to you over their first year?"

2. **Current handling —** "When a call comes in at lunch, after five, or on a Saturday, what happens to it right now? And how are you handling no-shows and last-minute cancellations today?"
   - *Listening for:* voicemail black-hole + manual no-show backfill. That's the cost of doing nothing.

3. **Decision-maker —** "If this is a fit and the numbers make sense, is it your call, or would you want your office manager in on it too?"
   - *Listening for:* Dr. Park + office manager. Get both on the day-12 call.

4. **Timeline —** "If we could have this live and catching every call inside two weeks, is now the time to fix it, or is something else ahead of it?"

5. **Budget authority —** "Most practices we work with were quietly losing well over this every month. When something pays for itself, can you greenlight it, or is there a budget conversation first?"

## 3 · The pilot close (signed agreement BEFORE the free pilot)

> ⚠️ **Mechanic:** signature **today**, payment **on conversion**.

"Here's how I'd get you started — totally transparent, zero surprises."

## 4 · Objection handling

**"I'm too busy right now."**
- "That's *exactly* why this exists — busy is what's making you miss calls, and missed calls are missed patients. And **we build it, you don't.** Fifteen minutes now buys your front desk hours back every week."

**"I already have a receptionist."**
- "Perfect — then you know what a missed call costs. Your receptionist takes lunch, handles one line at a time, and goes home at five. This catches the calls she can't — the second caller, the 7pm Saturday emergency — and books them. It backs her up; it doesn't replace her. What happens to a call right now at 7pm Saturday?"

**"I'm not sure about AI — will it sound robotic to my patients?"**
- "Fair — most AI is clunky. That's the whole point of the pilot: you don't judge my pitch, you judge it handling *your* real patient calls for two weeks, free. If it doesn't sound right, you don't convert."

**"It's too expensive."**
- "Let's check that against the leak. ~40 calls a week are slipping — at $450 a new patient, recovering even a couple a week covers the whole fee and then some. This isn't a new cost; it's a leak you're already paying, except the money just disappears. And you pay nothing until it's proven."

**"Is this HIPAA compliant? What about patient privacy?"** — *(Lead with BAA credibility — this is a buying signal.)*
- **Acknowledge + flip:** "Great question — the fact that you asked tells me you run a tight practice. It's the *first* thing we handle, not the last."
- **Evidence:** "We sign a **Business Associate Agreement (BAA)** with you before a single call is answered. Calls and messages are encrypted in transit and at rest, access is logged, and we're architected for HIPAA from the ground up — most answering services can't say that."
- **Re-close:** "So compliance isn't the risk — voicemail quietly losing patients is. Want me to include the BAA right in the agreement we sign today?"
