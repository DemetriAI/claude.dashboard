# Gamma deck — generic optometry (reusable)

Use when you don't have a personalized deck for a target yet. Swap `{{PRACTICE}}` / `{{METRO}}` /
`{{PAIN_BAND}}` and paste into **Gamma → Generate**. Problem-first. Card 1 = pain. One CTA.

---
```text
Create a 7-card presentation for a live 15-minute call with {{PRACTICE}}, an independent family
optometry practice in {{METRO}}. Tone: direct, warm, numbers-first, no hype. Problem-first — do NOT
open with our company or features. One call to action, last card only.

Card 1 — The leak: "{{PRACTICE}} is losing ~{{PAIN_BAND}} every month to missed phone calls."
After-hours, lunch, and second-callers roll to voicemail; in eye care a voicemail is a patient who
books the practice down the street. (Conservative estimate; confirmed together on the call.)

Card 2 — Why it persists: "It's a coverage problem, not a staff problem." One or two people can't
answer while with a patient, after close, or on a second line. Hiring doesn't fix nights/weekends.

Card 3 — The fix: "An AI front desk that answers every call and books it." 24/7, sounds like your
practice, handles routine questions, books straight into your calendar.

Card 4 — What 7 days looks like: "Live on your number in 7 days." Forward the line, train on your
services + scheduling, then it answers real calls. Recovered bookings show inside the week.

Card 5 — Proof: "You'll be the proof — on your own phones." The pilot runs on your real calls; you
watch the recovered bookings before you pay. Your calendar is the evidence.

Card 6 — Pilot terms: "Free 7-day pilot. Sign today; pay only if you continue." Built free, price
locked in the agreement you sign now, charged only if you keep it. Doesn't work → you walk, owe nothing.

Card 7 — One CTA: "Let's set it live this week." Pick a 15-minute slot; confirm number + scheduling;
answering within days. One button: Book the 15 minutes.
```
