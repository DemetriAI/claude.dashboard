## 0 · Teardown snapshot (read before you dial)

- **Call volume:** ~140 inbound/week (spikes hard in summer)
- **The leak:** techs on roofs/in crawlspaces all day → **~35 missed calls/wk in peak**; after-hours rings out; emergency "no-AC" calls go to whoever picks up first
- **No-show / reschedule handling today:** manual; missed-call callbacks often **next day — too late for an emergency**
- **What a booked job is worth:** ~$350 service call / $8,000+ install or replacement
- **Estimated monthly revenue leaking:** ~$12–16K/mo (lost service calls + at least 1–2 installs walking to competitors)
- **Hook line (their words):** "In July my guys are on roofs all day — nobody's on the phone, and I *know* we're losing installs to whoever answers first."

## 1 · Qualification (5 questions)

1. **Volume —** "Walk me through a busy summer week — how many calls come in, and how many do you figure ring out or hit voicemail while the crews are out?"
   - *Anchor:* "Teardown put it around 35 a week in peak — sound right?" → *Follow:* "What's an average service call worth? And a full system replacement?"

2. **Current handling —** "When a call comes in and every tech is on a job — or it's 9pm and somebody's AC just died — what happens to that call right now?"
   - *Listening for:* voicemail / next-day callback / lost to competitor. That's the bleed.

3. **Decision-maker —** "If this is a fit and the math works, is it your call, or is there a partner / GM you'd loop in?"

4. **Timeline —** "We're heading into your busiest stretch. If we could have this answering and booking inside two weeks, is now the time, or is something ahead of it?"
   - *Urgency is free here — sell into the summer peak.*

5. **Budget authority —** "Most shops we work with were losing way more than this in walked jobs every month. When something pays for itself, can you pull the trigger, or is there a budget step first?"

## 4 · Objection handling

**"I'm slammed — call me after the season."**
- "After the season is exactly when you'll have lost the most. Busy *is* the problem — the calls ringing out are booked jobs. And **we build it, you don't.** Let's get it live *for* the rush, not after it."

**"I've got an answering service already."**
- "Then you know they take a message — they don't *book the job* or screen the emergency. By the time you call back, the homeowner already hired whoever answered live. This books it on the spot, 24/7. What happens to a 9pm no-AC call right now?"

**"I'm not sure about AI — will it sound off to my customers?"**
- "Fair — most of it's rough. That's the pilot's whole job: you don't trust my word, you hear it handle *your* real calls for two weeks, free. Doesn't sound right? You don't convert."

**"It's too expensive."**
- "Against the leak? You're losing ~35 calls a week in peak — one saved install is $8K+, and the whole year of service is $597 a month. This isn't a cost, it's plugging a hole money's already pouring through. And you pay nothing till it's proven."

**"What happens to my customer / lead data — is it secure?"**
- **Acknowledge:** "Smart to ask — those leads *are* your revenue."
- **Evidence:** "Your data is yours, full stop — encrypted, never sold or shared, and we'll sign a mutual NDA if you want it in writing. Our infrastructure is actually healthcare-grade (BAA-ready), so it's held to a higher bar than this even needs."
- **Re-close:** "Data's locked down. The only thing leaking right now is the calls going to voicemail — ready to plug that?"
