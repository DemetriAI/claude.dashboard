# PremierConnect AI for Hedge Funds — Outreach & Sales Playbook

> The master brief for selling PremierConnect AI to Austin funds. Companion files: `offers.md` (the 4 offers), `targets.md` (30 firms + contacts + messages).

## 0. The strategic reframe (read first)
The real-estate "AI front desk" (instant lead response + database reactivation) does **not** transfer to hedge funds as-is — funds have no consumer lead flow. Their equivalent of "leads" is **capital**: prospective LPs/allocators. So we reposition the *same engine* around the one thing every fund obsesses over — **raising and retaining capital with less manual IR grind.**

**Product, repositioned — *PremierConnect AI: an AI-powered Investor Relations & capital-raising engine for emerging and lower-middle-market funds.***
- **Engine 1 — Speed-to-Capital:** every allocator inquiry, warm intro, and post-meeting / post-conference contact gets an instant, compliant, on-brand follow-up — so no LP relationship goes cold.
- **Engine 2 — Dormant-LP Reactivation:** systematically re-engage passed prospects, dormant LPs, and your network with compliant updates so re-ups and new commitments surface.
- **Plus — IR Ops Automation:** investor onboarding (sub-docs / KYC reminders), routine LP Q&A, meeting scheduling, reporting reminders, CRM hygiene — hours back for the GP/IR.

## 1. ICP — who to sell to
**Best fit (highest pain, fastest yes):**
- **Emerging / sub-$500M managers actively raising** — lean teams, no dedicated IR, hungry for AUM.
- Funds **launching a new vehicle** or **post-anchor and scaling**.
- Lean **family offices / multi-strategy shops** drowning in investor admin.

**Poor fit:** mega-funds with full IR departments and entrenched systems — sell *augmentation*, not replacement (or skip).

## 2. The promise & the outcome
- **Promise:** "Every dollar of interest gets followed up within the hour, every dormant LP gets re-engaged, and your IR admin runs itself — so you raise more, faster, with fewer hands."
- **Outcome they buy:** more committed capital (AUM), shorter raise cycles, zero dropped allocator relationships, dozens of IR hours back monthly. Fees scale with AUM, so **more AUM = more money**; **automation = hours saved.**

## 3. Pros & cons — what you're really selling
**Pros (why it lands):**
- Capital is existential; faster, consistent LP follow-up directly grows AUM.
- IR teams are tiny; automation frees the GP to invest and meet allocators.
- Reactivating passed / dormant LPs is "found money."
- 24/7, consistent, professional investor touchpoints.

**Cons / hard truths (address head-on):**
- **Regulation is the big one.** SEC Marketing Rule (206(4)-1), Reg D **506(b) vs 506(c)** (general-solicitation limits), no misleading performance claims, and **books-&-records / archiving** (Rule 204-2; 17a-4 analogs). → We build with **human-in-the-loop approval + archiving integration**, never auto-blast.
- **Do NOT act as an unregistered placement agent.** Transaction-based comp for introducing investors to a securities offering can require **broker-dealer registration.** → We sell **software + IR operations**, priced on subscription / hours-saved / qualified-engagement — **never a % of capital raised.**
- **Data security & confidentiality** — funds are paranoid. → NDA up front, SOC2 posture, least-privilege access.
- **Sophistication / skepticism** — many already run Dynamo, Backstop, Salesforce, Affinity. → We **integrate / augment**, not rip-and-replace.
- **Trust-based, long cycles** — relationships win. → Lead with a specific, credible, low-risk offer.

## 4. Onboarding (regulated-finance overlay on the `/fulfillment` engine)
1. **Day 0 — NDA + compliance alignment.** Sign NDA; review templates with their CCO; confirm 506(b)/506(c) status; set archiving (Global Relay / Smarsh) + approval workflow.
2. **Day 1 — Intake.** Connect CRM (Salesforce / Dynamo / Backstop / Affinity) or stand one up; import investor universe; segment **Current LPs / Prospects / Passed / Dormant / Network**; mark consent & relationship status.
3. **Days 2–3 — Build.** Speed-to-Capital follow-up, Dormant-LP reactivation, onboarding/KYC reminders, scheduling, reporting reminders — all with **approval gates + archiving.**
4. **Day 4 — Connect channels.** Compliant email footer, calendar, data-room links, conference/cap-intro follow-up templates.
5. **Day 5 — QA + compliance sign-off.** Live tests; CCO approves; archiving verified.
6. **Days 6–7 — Go-live + monitor.**

## 5. The outreach message framework ("the prompt")
Goal: book a 15-minute call **this week.** Kill "hey it's Demetri." Every message = **specific trigger about THEM → relevant pain → proof → tiny ask.**

**Personalization formula (per contact):**
1. **Trigger** — a true, recent, specific fact: new fund/vehicle, AUM milestone, key hire, 13F/Form ADV/Form D filing, conference talk, podcast/interview, LinkedIn post.
2. **Bridge** — connect it to an IR/capital pain ("scaling the raise," "lean IR team," "re-engaging LPs who passed on Fund I").
3. **Proof / Offer** — one of the 4 offers (results-based, risk-reversed).
4. **CTA** — one specific, low-friction ask: "Worth 15 minutes Thursday or Friday?"

**Reusable template:**
> "{{First}} — saw {{specific trigger about their fund}}. Most {{emerging managers / lean IR teams}} I work with hit the same wall right after: {{specific pain}}. We built an AI IR engine that {{outcome}} — e.g., {{one-line proof}}. Not a pitch, an offer: {{specific offer}}. Worth 15 minutes {{day/day}}?"

**Channel order to land a meeting this week:** (1) LinkedIn connect + note → (2) personalized email same day → (3) follow-up email +48h → (4) call if number known. Keep it 1:1 and compliant — you're selling **software / IR ops**, never soliciting securities.
