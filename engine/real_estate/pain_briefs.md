# Pain Briefs — real_estate (Agent 3)

Residential real-estate agents are some of the purest "speed-to-lead leaks ad spend" buyers in any
vertical. A solo agent or small team pays **$15–$50 per lead** on Meta/Google to drive a "What's my home
worth?" seller funnel or a buyer IDX funnel — then that hard-bought lead rings a single cell phone that's
busy at a showing, at a closing, or asleep. Speed-to-lead data is brutal: contact rates fall off a cliff
after the first 5 minutes, yet most agents' median response is measured in hours, so a large share of paid
leads are bought and then abandoned to voicemail. The stakes per lead are huge — buy-side commission on a
~$400K home runs **$6,000–$15,000** — so even one or two missed-call leads/month converting to a competitor
erases the entire ad budget and then some, putting **$3,000–$14,000/month** of value at risk for an
owner-operator (est., confirm on call). The wedge: these are exactly the people who own both the phone and
the ad budget but have no after-hours intake; mega-teams with staffed Inside Sales Agents (ISAs) and
franchise call-centers have already solved this (hence the HALT list).

**Guardrail (non-negotiable):** the AI is **lead intake, qualification (budget/timeline/financing), and
showing/consult scheduling ONLY** — it never screens or steers on protected classes (Fair Housing Act),
and gives no pricing, offer, or legal advice. It captures the lead instantly, qualifies neutrally, and
books the appointment so the ad spend stops leaking.
