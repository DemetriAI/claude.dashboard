# Cold Outreach — accounting (Agent 7)

Lead-with-pain, artifact-led. Every message opens with **their** pain + the **$ figure** (built from
**retained client $1,500–$5,000/yr**, or staff-hours for the document chase), has **one CTA** (book
15 min), and carries the **personalized Loom** as the value artifact. No spray-and-pray. **Guardrail
in every message:** the AI gives **no tax or financial advice** — intake, scheduling, and
doc-collection only — with **confidentiality + NDA** named before they ask.

**Rule of 100:** design for ~100 personalized touches/day — but *personalized* (each carries that
firm's Loom + pain band), never blasted. Quality of the artifact is the unlock.

**Daily mechanics:** ~10–15 fresh PROCEED targets/day from `leads.csv` (top score first) → record a
Loom from `looms/_TEMPLATE.md` → run the 5-touch cadence below.
Log replies to `pipeline.csv` → hand to bookings (Agent 9).

**Season timing (this vertical's edge):** the pitch lands hardest when the pain is near.
- **Nov–early Jan:** prime window — "live before January 15."
- **Aug–Sep:** second window — "live before the Oct 15 extension crunch."
- **Off-season (now):** sell calm — "the cheapest week to install this is one when the phone is quiet."
**Never** cold-call a CPA the first two weeks of April or the week of a filing deadline.

## The 5-touch cadence (per target, ~9 business days)
| Day | Channel | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Email #1** + Loom | Pain + $ figure + the free-pilot CTA (guardrail line included) |
| 2 | **Phone** (opener below) | Catch them live; reference the Loom |
| 4 | **LinkedIn** connect + 1-line note | Second surface, low friction |
| 6 | **Email #2** (bump) | Risk-reversal; "you only continue if it works" |
| 9 | **Email #3** (breakup) | Takeaway close; leaves the door open |

## Worked example A — [Firm], season call-spike lead (pain band ~$5,000–$15,000/yr)

**Email #1** — subject: `[[Firm]] — the March calls you never hear ring`
> `[[Owner]]` — from mid-January to April 15 your phone rings 3–4x normal, right when everyone's
> heads-down in returns. Those overflow and after-6 calls roll to voicemail — and a prospect who hits
> voicemail in March signs with the next firm. At **$1,500–$5,000/yr per retained client**, missing
> even 3–8 of them is **~$5,000–$15,000/yr** in recurring revenue, every season.
> I made you a 90-second teardown: `[[Loom link]]`.
> The fix: an AI front desk that answers every call 24/7, qualifies, and books the consult. It gives
> **no tax or financial advice** — intake, scheduling, doc-collection only — under NDA; your clients'
> data stays yours. I'll build it **free** and run it on your phones for 7 days — you only continue
> if it catches calls.
> Worth 15 minutes this week? `[[booking link]]`

**Phone opener (Day 2):**
> "Hi, this is `[[you]]` — I sent `[[owner]]` a quick 90-second video on the calls `[[Firm]]` misses
> during busy season. At retained-client values it's five figures a year in recurring revenue. I'm
> not selling anything today — I want 15 minutes to show the fix and offer to build it free. And
> before you ask: it gives no tax advice — intake, scheduling, and document collection only, under
> NDA. Is `[[owner]]` around, or when's good?"

**LinkedIn note (Day 4):**
> `[[Owner]]` — sent you a 90-sec teardown of `[[Firm]]`'s busy-season missed calls (~$5–15K/yr at
> retained-client values). No tax advice — intake + scheduling + doc-chase only, NDA up front. Happy
> to build the fix free for 7 days. Worth a look?

**Email #2 — bump (Day 6)** — subject: `re: [[Firm]]`
> Floating this back up. The whole point of the free 7-day pilot is you risk nothing — it runs on
> your real phones, under NDA, and you only pay if it's catching calls. Install it while things are
> quiet; it's ready before the next deadline crunch. 15 minutes? `[[booking link]]`

**Email #3 — breakup (Day 9)** — subject: `closing the loop`
> I'll stop here, `[[owner]]` — but January doesn't care either way: the spike comes, the overflow
> calls ring out, and they sign somewhere. If catching every busy-season call ever moves up your
> list, the free pilot stands. `[[booking link]]`
