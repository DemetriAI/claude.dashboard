# Cold Outreach — law (Agent 7)

Lead-with-pain, artifact-led. Every message opens with **their** pain + the **$ figure** (built from
**signed matter $3,500+**), has **one CTA** (book 15 min), and carries the **personalized Loom** as
the value artifact. No spray-and-pray. **Guardrail in every message:** the AI gives **no legal
advice** — intake + scheduling only — and we lead compliance with **confidentiality + privilege +
NDA** before they ask.

**Rule of 100:** design for ~100 personalized touches/day — but *personalized* (each carries that
firm's Loom + pain band), never blasted. Quality of the artifact is the unlock.

**Daily mechanics:** ~10–15 fresh targets/day from `leads.csv` (top score first) → record a Loom from
`looms/_TEMPLATE.md` → run the 5-touch cadence below. Log replies to `pipeline.csv` → hand to
bookings (Agent 9). **Timing note:** attorneys are in court mid-morning; phone touches land best
8:00–8:45am or 4:30–6:00pm local.

## The 5-touch cadence (per target, ~9 business days)
| Day | Channel | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Email #1** + Loom | Pain + $ figure + the free-pilot CTA (guardrail line included) |
| 2 | **Phone** (opener below) | Catch them live; reference the Loom |
| 4 | **LinkedIn** connect + 1-line note | Second surface, low friction |
| 6 | **Email #2** (bump) | Risk-reversal; "you only continue if it works" |
| 9 | **Email #3** (breakup) | Takeaway close; leaves the door open |

> **no legal advice** — intake + scheduling only — under NDA, built around confidentiality and privilege.
> I'll build it **free** and run it on your intake line for 7 days — you only continue if it catches calls.
> Worth 15 minutes this week? `[[booking link]]`

**Phone opener (Day 2):**
> "Hi, this is `[[you]]` — I sent Adam a quick 90-second video on the intake calls Loewy misses after
> hours and during depositions. At PI matter values it's five figures a month in signed cases. I'm not
> selling anything today — I want 15 minutes to show the fix and offer to build it free. And before
> you ask: it gives no legal advice, intake and scheduling only, under NDA. Is Adam around, or when's
> good?"

**LinkedIn note (Day 4):**
> Adam — sent you a 90-sec teardown of Loewy's missed-intake leak (~$7–25K/mo at PI matter values).
> Intake + scheduling only, no legal advice, NDA up front. Happy to build the fix free for 7 days.

**Email #2 — bump (Day 6)** — subject: `re: Loewy Law Firm`
> Floating this back up. The whole point of the free 7-day pilot is you risk nothing — it runs on your
> real intake line, under NDA, and you only pay if it's catching intakes. 15 minutes? `[[booking link]]`

**Email #3 — breakup (Day 9)** — subject: `closing the loop`
