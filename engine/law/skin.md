# Vertical Skin — law

> "Swap the config, not the engine." This is the re-skin: same 12 agents, law-specific pain
> hypotheses, channels, value math, and guardrails. Pains rhyme with optometry — it's the
> **missed-call → lost booking** pattern, except a "booking" is a **signed matter** worth far more.

## ICP / who we keep
Owner-operated small firm (solo–~10 attorneys) · high-intent inbound (personal injury, family,
criminal, estate, immigration) · the **attorney/firm owns the intake line** · intake-and-consult driven.
**HALT:** BigLaw, in-house legal depts, firms with a staffed 24/7 intake call center already.

## Pains (TIME or MONEY, quantified — est., confirm on the call)
| Rank | Pain | Economics (est.) | AI fix (intake/scheduling only) | First result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Missed/slow intake calls** → lost signed matters. Injury calls come 24/7; first firm to answer usually wins. | Signed matter $3,500+ (per `calls/_PLAYBOOK.md`). Recover ~2–6 intakes/mo → **~$7,000–$25,000/mo** | AI answers every intake 24/7, qualifies, books the consult | ≤7 days |
| 2 | **Slow web-lead response** | Speed-to-lead decides who signs; minutes vs. hours | AI calls/answers web leads instantly, books consult | ≤7 days |
| 3 | **Consult no-shows** | ~15–25% no-show; each is lost attorney time | Automated reminders + easy reschedule | ≤7 days |
| 4 | **After-hours coverage** | Nights/weekends = dead line today | AI intake runs 24/7 | ≤7 days |
| 5 | **Review leakage** | Thin reviews lose discovery to competitors | AI review request post-matter | ≤7 days |

## Off-limits / guardrails
- **The AI never gives legal advice.** It does intake and scheduling only — qualify, capture facts,
  book the consult. Crossing into advice is both wrong and a liability landmine.
- Lead the compliance objection with **confidentiality + privilege + NDA up front** (mirror the legal
  cheat-sheet in `calls/_PLAYBOOK.md`).
- Conflict-check stays with the firm; the AI flags, it doesn't clear.

## Channels & agent deltas
- **Cold (Agent 7):** email + LinkedIn + phone, Loom-led. Viable.
- **Warm (Agent 6):** bar-association / referral networks are strong.
- **Ads (Agent 8):** viable for PI/consumer law once the 2-client gate is met (high case value = ad math works).
- **Value per unit (Agents 2/3/5/10):** signed matter $3,500+; lead with matter value, not call count.
- **Close (downstream):** `./calls/_new-prep.sh "<Firm>" legal`.
