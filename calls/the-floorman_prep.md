## 1 · Qualification (5 questions — disqualify fast, or load the close)

Ask, then **shut up and write**. Each answer feeds the value math in §2.

1. **Volume —** "Walk me through a typical week — roughly how many calls come in, and honestly, how many do you think hit voicemail or never get answered?"
   - *Listening for:* the size of the leak. → *Follow:* "And what's one booked customer worth to you, ballpark?"

2. **Current handling —** "When a call comes in after hours, or while your team's slammed, what happens to it right now? And how are you handling job no-shows and cancellations today?"
   - *Listening for:* voicemail, missed calls, manual scramble, nothing. This is the cost of doing nothing.

3. **Decision-maker —** "If this turns out to be a fit and the numbers make sense, is it your call to move forward, or is there anyone else you'd want in the room?"
   - *Listening for:* every name that can say yes — or no. Get them on this call if you can.

4. **Timeline —** "If we could have this live and capturing calls for you inside two weeks, is now the right time to fix this — or is there something that has to happen first?"
   - *Listening for:* urgency and readiness. Surfaces hidden blockers before the close.

5. **Budget authority —** "Most customers we work with were quietly losing far more than this costs every month. When something pays for itself, can you greenlight it, or is there a budget conversation that has to happen first?"
   - *Listening for:* can they authorize setup fee + MRR. If "no," loop in the person who can (back to Q3).

## 2 · Value framing — Hormozi value equation

```
            Dream Outcome  ×  Perceived Likelihood
  VALUE  =  ──────────────────────────────────────
              Time Delay   ×   Effort & Sacrifice
            ▲ maximize these        ▼ crush these
```

| Lever | What you say |
|---|---|
| **Dream outcome** ▲ | "Every call answered and every after-hours emergency booked — crews running full, and not one job walking to the competitor because nobody picked up." |
| **Likelihood** ▲ | "You don't have to take my word for it — an HVAC company captured 31 after-hours calls in 14 days → 11 booked jobs → 2 system replacements (~$18K). And the pilot itself is the proof: you watch it work on *your* phones before you ever pay." |
| **Time delay** ▼ | "We're live in **14 days** — not next quarter. You see real results inside the pilot window." |
| **Effort & sacrifice** ▼ | "**We build the whole thing.** Your team does nothing — no scripts to write, no software to learn, no new hire to train. You approve it and it runs." |

**The synthesis line (say it out loud):**
> "So you get [dream], proven on your own phones, live in two weeks, and you don't lift a finger to build it. The pilot is free — the only way to lose here is to keep doing nothing."

## 3 · The pilot close (signed agreement BEFORE the free pilot)

> ⚠️ **Non-negotiable mechanic:** signature **today**, payment **on conversion**. The free pilot is the risk-reversal; the signed agreement is the commitment that locks price and makes production the default.

**Transition into the close** *(assumptive, calm):*
"Here's how I'd get you started — and I'm going to be totally transparent so there are zero surprises."

**Lay out the four steps** *(count them on your fingers — live closers, slow down here):*
1. "**Today, we sign the agreement.** It locks your setup fee at **$1,500** and your monthly at **$597/mo** — that price is held, it won't move."
2. "The minute you sign, **my team starts building.** Nothing is charged. Zero."
3. "You run it **free for 14 days** — real calls, your customers, your dispatch board. You watch it work."
4. "On day 14, if it's doing what we said — capturing calls and booking jobs — it **converts to production, and *that's* when the setup fee and first month bill.** If it's somehow not a fit, you don't convert, and you owe nothing."

**The lock** *(then stop talking):*
> "So — you're signing today so we can start building now and hold your pricing. But you don't pay a dollar until you've seen it work on your own phones. That's about as fair as it gets. Want me to get the agreement over to you now?"

[Silence. The next person to speak owns the deal. Let it be them.]

**If yes →** send agreement, confirm signature live if possible, then go straight to §5 and lock the day-12 call. **Do not leave the call without the conversion call booked.**

## 4 · Objection handling (acknowledge → reframe → evidence → re-close)

**"I'm too busy / now's not a good time."**
- "That's *exactly* why this exists — it's busy that's making you miss calls, and missed calls are missed customers. And remember: **we build it, you don't.** The busiest week you have is the week this pays for itself. Fifteen minutes now buys your team back hours every week."

**"I already have a receptionist / answering service."**
- "Perfect — then you already know what a missed call costs. Your receptionist sleeps, takes lunch, handles one call at a time, and calls in sick. This catches every call they can't — nights, weekends, the second and third caller — and books them. It doesn't replace your team; it stops the leak around the edges. What happens to calls right now at 7pm on a Saturday?"

**"I'm not sure about AI / will it sound robotic?"**
- "Totally fair — most AI *is* clunky. That's the whole point of the pilot: you don't decide based on my pitch, you decide based on hearing it handle *your* real calls for two weeks, free. If it doesn't sound right, you don't convert. When's the last time a vendor let you try the finished product on your own customers before paying?"

**"It's too expensive / what's it cost?"**
- "Let's check that against the leak. You said about 5–10 high-value jobs/mo are slipping through — at $350 service call / $8,000+ install or replacement, even recovering a *handful* covers the whole fee and then some. This isn't a cost, it's a leak you're already paying for, except right now the money just disappears. And you pay nothing until it's proven. What number would make this a no-brainer?"

**"What happens to my customer / lead data — is it secure?"**
- **Acknowledge:** "Smart to ask — those leads *are* your revenue."
- **Evidence:** "Your data is yours, full stop — encrypted, never sold or shared, and we'll sign a mutual NDA if you want it in writing. Our infrastructure is actually healthcare-grade (BAA-ready), so it's held to a higher bar than this even needs."
- **Re-close:** "Data's locked down. The only thing leaking right now is the calls going to voicemail — ready to plug that?"
