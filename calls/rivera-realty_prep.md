## 1 · Qualification (5 questions)

1. **Volume + speed —** "Walk me through a typical week — how many new leads come in across Zillow, your site, Facebook? And honestly, when one comes in while you're at a showing, how long before someone actually replies?"
   - *Anchor:* "Industry average is 15+ hours, and most buyers go with whoever answers first — where do you think you really land?" → *Follow:* "What's a closed buyer or listing side worth to you on average?"

2. **Current handling + database —** "When a lead comes in at 9pm or while you're mid-showing, what happens to it right now? And your past clients and sphere — how are you staying in front of them between deals?"
   - *Listening for:* leads to voicemail / slow text **and** zero database nurture. Both are the bleed — and the database is the quiet one nobody else is selling her on.

3. **Decision-maker —** "If this is a fit, is it your call, or is there a partner / team member you'd want in on it?"

4. **Timeline —** "If we could have every lead answered in under a minute *and* your database working itself inside two weeks, is now the time to fix this, or is something ahead of it?"

5. **Budget authority —** "One recovered buyer pays for a year of this several times over. When something pays for itself, can you greenlight it, or is there a conversation first?"

## 4 · Objection handling

**"I'm showing houses all day — I don't have time for this."**
- "That's the exact problem — you being unreachable at showings is *when* the portal leads call the next agent. And **we build it, you don't.** Fifteen minutes now stops buyers walking while you're in a listing."

**"I already pay for leads (Zillow / Realtor.com)."**
- "Then you've already felt the leak — paying $200–500 a lead and losing them because the reply comes hours later. This answers them in under a minute, so the leads you *already bought* actually convert. And the bigger win is free: your past-client database — ~82% of deals are repeat/referral, and right now that asset's going untouched. What happens to a 9pm Zillow lead today?"

**"I'm not sure about AI — will it sound robotic to my leads?"**
- "Fair — most AI is clunky. That's the whole point of the pilot: you don't trust my word, you hear it handle *your* real leads for two weeks, free. If the tone's not right for your brand, you don't convert."

**"It's too expensive."**
- "Against one commission? A single recovered buyer is ~$7,500 — that's most of a year of the monthly in one close. You're losing at least a couple of buyers a year to slow response, plus several repeat/referral deals you never ask for. Recovering *one* pays for the year. And you pay nothing until it's proven."

**"Is texting my leads and clients even legal — what about TCPA / spam rules?"** — *(Lead with TCPA credibility — like the BAA question in healthcare, this is a buying signal.)*
- **Acknowledge + flip:** "Great question — the fact that you ask it tells me you protect your name in this market. Compliance is the *first* thing we build in, not the last."
- **Evidence:** "Every message runs on a **TCPA-compliant** framework: we text only leads who opted in or contacted you first, every send carries clear opt-out language, and opt-outs are honored automatically and logged. Your contacts stay yours — encrypted, never sold or shared — NDA on request. And no HIPAA/BAA hoops, so we're live faster than a medical build."
- **Re-close:** "So compliance is handled and on the record — the thing actually costing you is leads going cold before you reply. Want me to bake the consent + opt-out flow right into the agreement we sign today?"
