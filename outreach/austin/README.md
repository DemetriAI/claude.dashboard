## Free-value strategy — how to stand out from "AI connoisseurs"

Everyone else pitches. You **give first**, and you make it tangible:

1. **Hyper-specific opener** — one true, researched line about *them* (the hooks above). Proves it's not mass mail.
2. **A free, no-obligation audit** — *with their permission*, you measure their real inbound response time + database re-engagement and show exactly where commissions leak. They keep the findings even if they never reply. (Honest — you ask first; you do **not** submit fake inquiries.)
3. **A tangible asset they keep** — a **QR code / link to the interactive demo** (the `demo/` build) showing the system running on real-estate leads. High-effort, memorable, and it *shows* instead of tells.
4. **Total risk reversal** — free 14-day pilot on their own leads; you build it; they pay nothing until it converts.
5. **One CTA** — 15 minutes. Scan the code, or text/call/email.

> **Killer move:** host the `demo/` build, generate a QR per recipient, and print it on the letter. A physical letter with a QR to a custom live demo is something none of the "AI guys" are doing.
