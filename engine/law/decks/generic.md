# Gamma deck — generic law (reusable)

Use when you don't have a personalized deck for a target yet. Swap `{{FIRM}}` / `{{METRO}}` /
`{{PRACTICE_AREA}}` / `{{PAIN_BAND}}` and paste into **Gamma → Generate**. Problem-first. Card 1 =
pain. One CTA. Guardrail card (4) is mandatory.

---
```text
Create a 7-card presentation for a live 15-minute call with {{FIRM}}, an owner-operated
{{PRACTICE_AREA}} firm in {{METRO}}. Tone: direct, plain-spoken, numbers-first, no hype. Problem-first
— do NOT open with our company or features. One call to action, last card only.

Card 1 — The leak: "{{FIRM}} is losing ~{{PAIN_BAND}} every month in signed matters."
Intake calls land after hours, during court, and mid-consult — and they roll to voicemail. In
{{PRACTICE_AREA}}, the caller doesn't leave a message; they dial the next firm, and the first firm
to answer usually signs. A signed matter is $3,500+ — often far more. (Conservative estimate;
confirmed together on the call.)

Card 2 — Why it persists: "It's a coverage problem, not a staff problem." A solo or small firm
cannot answer while in court, in mediation, or after 6pm — and hiring an intake team doesn't fix
nights, weekends, or the second simultaneous caller.

Card 3 — The fix: "An AI intake line that answers every call and books the consult." 24/7, sounds
like your firm, qualifies the caller, captures the matter details, books straight into your
calendar. Every intake gets caught — the ones you're missing today included.

Card 4 — Guardrails first: "No legal advice. Ever." The AI does intake + scheduling only — it never
gives a legal opinion. NDA signed before we touch your line. Built around confidentiality and
privilege. Conflict checks stay with your team: it flags, your attorneys clear.

Card 5 — What 7 days looks like: "Live on your number in 7 days." Forward the line, train it on your
practice areas + scheduling rules, then it answers real intake calls. Captured intakes show inside
the week — no IT project, no new hire.

Card 6 — Pilot terms + proof: "Free 7-day pilot. You'll be the proof — on your own intake line."
Built free, price locked in the agreement you sign now, charged only if you keep it after the pilot
proves itself. If it doesn't catch intakes, you walk, owe nothing. The evidence is your own consult
calendar.

Card 7 — One CTA: "Let's set it live this week." Pick a 15-minute slot; we confirm your number,
practice areas, and scheduling rules; it's answering within days. One button: Book the 15 minutes.
```
---
*VERIFY: Card 1 = the pain (signed-matter $) · one CTA card · guardrail card present (no legal
advice + confidentiality/privilege/NDA) · 7 cards · no alpha claims (N/A).*
